Deepening Mathematical Understanding

How should I help students get started?

Mathematically rich tasks often intentionally lack specificity about how students should approach them. That ambiguity can actually help to build problem-solving skills and to facilitate mathematical transfer; but it can also leave students overwhelmed and unsure of how to move forward (or even get started).
